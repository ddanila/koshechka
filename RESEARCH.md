# Sources and further reading

Research collected 12–14 November 2026. These are external links, not copies of the historical works or a grant of rights to redistribute them. The [full link catalogue](research/catalogue.json) also lists the individual exhibition photographs, publisher preview images, mirrors and unsuccessful retrievals found during the research.

## Start here

- [Mathematical Etudes: «Кошечка»](https://etudes.ru/etudes/cat-animation/) — the original film, historical introduction, stills and technical paper.

## Original paper, film and first-person history

**Н. Н. Константинов, В. В. Минахин, В. Ю. Пономаренко. «Программа, моделирующая механизм и рисующая мультфильм о нем».** *Проблемы кибернетики* 28 (1974), pp.193–209. Received 25 December 1972; the film dates to 1968. [DjVu](https://etudes.ru/.data/localdocs/koshechka.djvu), [MCCME mirror](https://old.mccme.ru/shen/konst/publications/koshechka.djv). The mirrors contain the same scan. The article describes an algorithm, not an original source-code listing, and explicitly omits the numerical shape lists.

The film is also available as an [Etudes MP4](https://etudes.ru/.data/video/EtudesRu_cat-animation.mp4) and [MCCME AVI](https://old.mccme.ru/shen/konst/publications/Cat.avi). The retrieved film runs about 84 seconds. Artist-created grimaces and arched-back scenes should not be used to infer the computer's leg controller.

**Леонид Левкович-Маслюк, *Компьютерра* №7 (627), 21 February 2006:** [magazine extract](https://old.mccme.ru/shen/konst/publications/koshechka.pdf), [«Прибытие “Кошечки”»](https://www.kinnet.ru/cterra/627/254339.html), and [interview: «Знает ли кошка, что она не настоящая?»](https://old.mccme.ru/edu/konst/Computerra_cat2-2006.html). Konstantinov recalls the gait's development, the handmade additions, changes of institution and related molecular-animation work. These are recollections decades after the project.

[«Кошечка» / «Вклад в компьютерную анимацию», *Математическое образование* 40 (2007), pp.5–8](https://old.mccme.ru/shen/konst/publications/5-8.pdf) reprints introductory and interview material. It does not replace the full 1974 technical article.

## Historical programming and libraries

These public sources help explain the programming environment around M-20 and
BESM-4. They do not establish which library version was used for the cat. The
summaries below come from published books, papers and catalogues; they contain
no transcription or analysis of the unpublished original cat source code.

### Kronrod and the Б-61 library

**А. С. Кронрод, *Беседы о программировании*, 2nd stereotyped edition, Едиториал
УРСС, 2004.** [Public scan](https://is.ifmo.ru/books/cronrod.pdf). Page numbers below
are printed folios; in these sections the PDF page is one less.

| Printed pages | What the book documents |
|---|---|
| 51–61 | Standard-library organization, shared argument/result cells and fixed entry headers. Page 54 places M-20 Б-61 headers at **7600–7766 (octal)**, while routine bodies may move. |
| 73 | FA address formation on the modified ITEP M-20: offsets come from the middle address fields of index words; the stored following instruction remains unchanged. |
| 229, reference 1 | **«Библиотека Б-61. Ч. I. Описание и инструкции. Ч. II. Программы». ИТЭФ, 1965.** A precise lead for library documentation and program listings. |
| 236–239 | M-20 instruction summary: 45-bit words, three 12-bit addresses and address-register modification. This is an M-20 specification, not a complete BESM-4 manual. |

The two-part **1965 Б-61 preprint** is a priority acquisition target. We have its
bibliographic reference, not the two parts themselves. A fixed header range alone
does not identify individual routines or establish use by the cat program.

binaries. Б-61 and ИС-22 remain separate research leads.

### Two 1964 papers: notation and address formation

- **А. Л. Брудно, «Программирование в содержательных обозначениях».** *Доклады АН
  СССР* 154:2 (1964), pp.279–282. [Record and full text](https://www.mathnet.ru/rus/dan29026).
  Explains semantic notation alongside encoded instructions and a memory
  dictionary. Its example machine is hypothetical; the example opcodes are not
  a BESM-4 instruction table.
- **Г. М. Адельсон-Вельский, А. Л. Брудно, А. С. Кронрод, П. Т. Резниковский,
  «О системе команд для трехадресной машины без регистра адреса».** *Доклады АН
  СССР* 154:3 (1964), pp.545–548. [Record and full text](https://www.mathnet.ru/rus/dan29078).
  Proposes a 43-bit machine whose FA operation uses the **third** address field
  of index words. This differs from Kronrod's middle-field convention. The shared
  operation name does not make these machine designs equivalent.

### Hardware documentation and emulation

[**БЭСМ-4, часть III: перечень и описание элементов блоков**](https://retro-computer.ru/Lists/DocumentList/DispForm.aspx?ID=965)
is a hardware scan with 74 PDF spreads corresponding to a catalogue description
of 147 printed pages. The printer-driver and drum-write sections describe
electronic circuitry. They do not supply a character-code table, a verified Ш
glyph, or a library interface. Component pulse frequencies must not be treated
as film frame rates.

The public [**rcgoff/m20 emulator**](https://github.com/rcgoff/m20) is a fork of
Dmitry Stefankov's M-20 emulator. It is a useful starting point for studying that
machine; adapting it to a particular BESM-4 installation requires evidence for
the differences. It is an external project with its own licensing, not part of
this repository's MIT implementation.

The most useful further finds would be the Б-61 volumes, the 1967 ИС-22
instruction, and documented library entry tables with argument, result and
return conventions. These public findings do not recover the numerical cat shape
lists omitted from the 1974 animation paper.

## Related mathematics and animation

These are context or comparative work, not additional recovered code for Кошечка.

- **Cyrus Levinthal, *Molecular Model-Building by Computer* (1966).** [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5930597/), [Stanford-hosted scan](https://web.stanford.edu/class/sbio228/public/readings/Molecular_Simulation_I_Lecture4/Levinthal_SCIAM_66_Protein_folding.pdf), [computer-animation history text](https://content-animation.org.uk/htmls/levinthal.htm). Corresponds to the English original of a Russian reference cited by the cat paper; edition pagination differs.
- **В. Б. Суханов, *Общая система симметричной локомоции наземных позвоночных и особенности передвижения низших тетрапод* (1968).** [MSU Zoological Museum DjVu](https://zmmu.msu.ru/morphology/library/Sukhanov_1968.djvu). Comparative locomotion, cited in the original bibliography.
- **Glen Speckert, *A Computerized Look at Cat Locomotion or One Way to Scan a Cat*, MIT AI Memo 374 (1976).** [MIT record](https://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/6251), [Bitsavers PDF](https://bitsavers.trailing-edge.com/pdf/mit/ai/aim/AIM-374.pdf). Analysis of real treadmill-cat motion; useful for future trajectory measurement, but later and independent.
- **Charles Csuri and James Shaffer, *Hummingbird* (1967).** [Ohio State archive](https://uas.osu.edu/charles-csuri). Earlier computer-animation context.
- **Tony Pritchett, *The Flexipede* (shown in 1968).** [Computer Conservation Society history](https://www.computerconservationsociety.org/resurrection/res74.htm). A contemporary character-animation comparison.
- [SIGGRAPH historical computer-animation filmography (1992), PDF](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/SVR-Issue-80-1992-Historical-Computer-Animation.pdf) — broader chronology. Avoid unqualified claims that Кошечка was the first computer animation of any kind.
- [Prometheus archive: «Движение 1»](https://archive.prometheuskzn.ru/our-archive/dvizhenie-1/) — gait-related historical material; its caption does not establish a second independent technical paper.

The original article also cites Arnoldi on Disney, Novikov on mathematical logic, Zhurkin/Lysov on DNA animation, and Orlovsky/Severin/Shik on dog locomotion. We did not locate verified complete online copies of those specific cited publications. Their citations can be checked in the 1974 paper's bibliography; no guessed download links are supplied here.
